Shipping, returns, and the questions we hear most.

Shipping

Every order ships free. Standard delivery is 5 to 8 business days inside the US and 7 to 14 days everywhere else. You will get a tracking note when the parcel leaves the studio. Orders placed on this demo are not shipped.

Returns & exchanges

Unworn items with tags can come back within 30 days. Exchanges for another size are free. Start from your order email, or write to hello@veora.example and include the order number.
